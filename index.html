Born in ’98 in Bormio, a small village between the Alps, I grew up surrounded by snow, steep slopes and twisty roads that shaped my childhood.

Full-time snowboarder, part-time skateboarder, and avid traveller with a big love for car racing and music, better if mixed together.

After high school that curiosity led me to Milan, where I studied Communication Design at Politecnico di Milano, with six months at Centro. in CDMX, Mexico in between.

Since 2023 I’ve been running Purple Studio, a collective I co-founded with a fellow videographer, working on projects that range from events to visual direction or simply getting that work done.
